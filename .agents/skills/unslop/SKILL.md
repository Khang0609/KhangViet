# Unslop

Edit text to remove AI patterns. Scan for the patterns below, then rewrite. Preserve meaning and match the intended tone.

1. No trailing "-ing" phrases that add no fact ("highlighting...", "ensuring...").
2. No vague attributions ("experts believe"). Name the source or delete.
3. No AI vocabulary: additionally, crucial, delve, enhance, foster, landscape, pivotal, showcase, testament, underscore, vibrant.
4. Say "is" or "has", not "serves as", "stands as", "boasts", "features".
5. No "not just X, but Y". State the point.
6. Don't force ideas into threes.
7. One name per thing. Repeat it.
8. No em dashes, en dashes, or hyphens used as dashes where a period or comma works.
9. Don't bold every proper noun or acronym.
10. No bold label and colon that restates its line ("**Performance:** Performance improved...").
11. No chatbot phrases ("I hope this helps!", "Let me know if...").
12. No praise or agreement openers. Respond directly.
13. Cut filler. "In order to" becomes "to". Delete "it is important to note that".
14. No generic conclusions. State specific plans or facts.
15. No abstract metaphor nouns: substrate, wedge, vector, nexus, primitive, harness, surface, scaffolding, paradigm, ratchet, north star, flywheel. Use the concrete word.
16. Say what it does, not how it feels. Name the mechanism or the number.
17. Active voice. Cut adverbs or use a stronger verb. Give the number.
18. Use the plain word: "use", not "utilize" or "leverage".
